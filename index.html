A Xianxia Shrine Defense

\1

“Patriarch, bad news, a clansman is injured! Rush him to the Divine Tree!”
“Patriarch, a clansman just died! Rush him to the Divine Tree before the corpse turns cold!”

  • Click the tree to draw in ambient Qi (Vitality). Switch the tree click to collect all Qi in the Shrine Actions panel. Hunters' sacrifices of ferocious beasts also feed your roots.
  • Clan Duties: farmers irrigate the Blood Spring terraces (Blood Rice), devotees burn incense (Faith), hunters bring beast blood (Vitality), and warriors guard the shrine. Spend Blood Rice to recruit warriors or trade at Yong'an.
  • World Deduction (25 Vitality) is a 20-second trial. Grow a central sapling's roots toward drifting gold and blue orbs while avoiding red hazard zones, and survive to choose 1 of 3 insights: a new art (such as One Leaf Obscuring Sight or Reversed Pill Circulation), an art upgrade, or a permanent blessing.
  • Send Dream: a tree cannot speak, but it can visit dreams. Warn the elders of the next raid, teach Chen Qingyu the Locust Sword, or comfort the whole clan.
  • Casualties: when a clansman falls, a countdown appears over them. Click to heal them at the Divine Tree (15 Vitality). If they die, click the body before the corpse turns cold to bind its soul into an Ancestral Soul Tablet (10 Faith). It then rises as a Yin Soldier in every raid.
  • Day and night: sunlight quickens rice, beast blood and the tree's growth, while moonlight deepens Faith and empowers the Yin Soldiers. The 1×/2× button speeds up time.
  • Raids begin when Raid Danger fills. Click the ground for Root Spikes, and use 1–8 for your arts.
  • Three threat levels: the Li & Liu mountain bandits, then the Four Great Clans of Yong'an City (Shi, Xie, Gan, Shen) with their espionage shamans and totem mercenaries, and finally the Blood-, Qi- and Spirit-eating Totems. Destroy the Spirit-eating Totem to become the Incense Tree God.
